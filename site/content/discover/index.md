## How a discovery works

Every discovery runs the same four steps, and each takes about a minute.

1. **Predict.** Commit to an answer before you read anything. A wrong guess teaches more than a right one.
2. **Observe.** One link opens the simulation at the place that shows the answer, and a picture beside it works without 3D.
3. **Explain.** The idea in plain language, with the equation one click away.
4. **Change a parameter.** A calculator preset to Concordia's own numbers, so you can break the answer and watch it move.

The questions are the ones a visitor asks first: the ones where Concordia's answer is surprising until you see the arithmetic. None needs more than school physics, and every number comes from the station's own fact sheet. Take them in any order.

The calculators are calculators. They do not change the simulation, and each page links to the matching view so you can see the result there. For a guided story rather than questions, start with [the journey](/journey/), and for every place you can open, see the [scene atlas](/scenes/).
