Concordia's pages describe the station as if it were 2045. The studies here are different. They are written today, as an engineer would write them for a design review, and they decide questions the station's canon has not settled yet. A study that survives review becomes a design note and then a page in the guide.

## How to review {#how}

- **Check the physics first.** Every study lists its assumptions and tags each claim as established physics, engineering extrapolation, speculative technology or fictional design choice.
- **Rerun the numbers.** Each study names a script in the [repository](https://github.com/DavidVeksler/concordia/tree/main/scripts/analysis) that prints every table. If your result differs, that is the most useful feedback there is.
- **Send corrections.** Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/DavidVeksler/concordia/issues/new/choose) with the study name, the section and what you found. Better sources, missing failure modes and wrong assumptions are all welcome.
- **Rules of evidence.** A figure from a vendor is marked as a vendor claim. A figure that has not been checked against the primary source is listed as unverified.
