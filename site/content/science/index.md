Concordia is real engineering applied at city scale. Each system below is built from physics you can check, and each page shows the work.

## How the pages work

Every page opens with the idea in everyday terms: a bucket swung overhead, a bicycle wheel, a barrel hoop. Then it shows how Concordia applies the idea, with numbers, and what it feels like to live with. A Go deeper block holds the equations and a worked example using Concordia's own figures. Real-world claims carry a source at the bottom of the page.

Nothing needs math to follow. The deeper blocks are there for students and engineers who want to check the arithmetic.

## The fourteen pages

- [Artificial gravity](/science/artificial-gravity/): how spin makes weight, and why it fades toward the axis.
- [Counter-rotation and momentum](/science/counter-rotation/): why the rotors spin in opposite pairs.
- [Structure and pressure](/science/structure/): the vessel, the megaframe and the end walls.
- [Radiation and debris](/science/radiation-and-debris/): the shield stack, storm shelters and the watch for rocks.
- [Air, water and food](/science/air-water-food/): the loops that keep a city alive.
- [Light and sky](/science/light-and-sky/): an artificial sky and its day lengths.
- [Power and heat](/science/power-and-heat/): solar platforms, HVDC and radiators.
- [Moving around](/science/moving-around/): metro, ascenders, spin transfer and the spine express.
- [Flying in a spinning world](/science/gliding/): fly a glider in a rotor, drop a ball and stand still in space.
- [Materials](/science/materials/): regolith, asteroid volatiles and the stores.
- [Mining the Moon and asteroids](/science/mining/): what still comes from Earth, the bag haulers, and lunar rock at scale.
- [Getting there](/science/getting-there/): L5, the launcher, the 5 day Earth transfer and the transfer station.
- [Skyhook](/science/skyhook/): a rotating tether that swaps speed between two spaceplanes and halves the entry speed.
- [Safety](/science/safety/): what changed since the 1970s, and why no single failure reaches everyone.
