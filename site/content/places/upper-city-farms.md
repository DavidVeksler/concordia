## Inside a space farm: the grow hall {#what-you-see}

The farm of Tsiolkovsky (Rotor 1) sits on the 1,000 m ring level. The floor is 1,500 m from the axis, so gravity is 0.60 g. A link bridge connects it to the ascender, and the visitor gallery looks along a deck of the grow hall.

The hall is 288 m long, 124 m wide and 96 m tall: 24 bays of 12 m and 12 decks of 8 m. Racks of lettuce, basil and kale stand in four growth stages, from seedlings to heads ready to cut. LED rigs light them, nutrient channels feed them, and a harvest gantry runs overhead.

{% shot "pl-farm-harvest-line" "Totes of greens on the belt through the wash tunnel, optical sorter and weigher." %}

Next to the hall is the process wing. Totes arrive on a belt and pass the wash tunnel, the optical sorter and the weigher. The wing also holds packing, a bakery, bottling and a cold store. Four {% term "aeroponics" "aeroponic" %} towers, 15 m across and 72 m tall, grow roots in mist. A fermentation block makes protein and ingredients, and a tank farm stores the water and nutrients.

{% shot "pl-farm-fringe" "Stacked crop galleries, nutrient vessels and robotic harvesters in the upper city." %}

Ordinary food leaves on freight pods from the dock: crates of salad, trays of loaves, bottles and wrapped pallets. The fermenters are about 50 m tall and make roughly 2 t of product an hour. Fermentation supplies protein and other ingredients alongside the crops.

## Why farm at 0.6 g {#why-up-here}

Partial gravity is preferred to none for growing, and a food factory does not need to be near the streets. Putting farms at 0.4 to 0.6 g keeps the 1 g ground for parks, schools and homes. Above about 800 m the frame is an atmospheric service district, not housing: utilities, crop galleries and process halls.

{% shot "sc-facility-1000m-exterior" "The 1,000 m service tier from the open fringe: glazed grow galleries lit white and magenta, with vessels and air handlers. Weight here is 0.60 g." %}

The air in the farm is the shared habitat air, so there are no airlocks and no separate farm vessel. Water and nutrients circulate in a {% term "closed-loop life support" "recycling loop" %}, with continuous recovery rather than a perfect seal. The science is in [Air, water and food](/science/air-water-food/), and the gravity story is in [Artificial gravity](/science/artificial-gravity/).

{% callout "In 2045" %}
Children and routine healthcare stay near 1 g, so the farms are workplaces first ([why](/station/building-concordia/)).
{% endcallout %}

Water is the other loop. The farms recover it continuously, and what slips away is topped up from the asteroid ice held in Ehricke, [the industrial rotor](/places/industrial-rotor/).

## The food loop {#food-loop}

The grow hall is one end of the city's food loop. Nitrate from the sewer lane and from the fish tanks feeds the decks. What the crops leave, stalks, roots and trimmings, feeds black soldier fly larvae, and larval meal and air protein feed the tilapia and the hens in the halls beside the grow hall. Their sludge and manure go back to the digesters. Power is cheap enough here that protein can be grown from hydrogen and carbon dioxide, and only about 2 percent of the city's food comes from Earth, most of it wine, coffee and other luxuries. The full chain is in [from sewer to supper](/science/air-water-food/#from-sewer-to-supper). {% basis "design" %}

{% deeper "Facility levels and methods" %}
Three levels of the frame carry service uses. At 625 m (0.75 g), one slot in four on the plate quarters is a utility plant for reclaimed water, nutrients and ventilation. At 1,000 m (0.60 g) are the crop galleries and process halls. At 1,500 m (0.40 g) are specialist process and crop research halls.

The farms use {% term "hydroponics" %}, aeroponics, stacked crops, fungi, algae, precision fermentation, microbial protein, insect larvae, fish tanks and hen barns. The grow hall's twelve decks give about 500,000 m² of growing area.

Every flow is tracked by element. There is no landfill: water, carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus, metals, glass and polymers are all recovered.
{% enddeeper %}
