## A tour from the ground up

Gravity is the map. The farther you are from the axis, the more you weigh, so the tour runs from the heaviest places to the lightest and then out into space.

{% shots "pl-arbor-street" "The heavy end of the tour: a street at the ground, where you weigh 1 g." "pl-spine-interior" "The light end: the stationary spine on the axis, where nothing weighs anything." %}

1. [Arbor Quarter](/places/arbor-quarter/) is a groundside neighbourhood at 1 g: streets, a lake park and the metro.
2. [The capital quarter](/places/capital-quarter/) is Noordung's Exchange, Charter Hall and Court, where the station prices its energy and keeps its law.
3. [The megaframe city](/places/megaframe-city/) is the second city on plates and bridges above the streets, and the inhabited cliffs of the end walls.
4. [Ascenders and the sky](/places/ascenders-and-sky/) is the ten-minute lift through the artificial sky.
5. [Upper city farms](/places/upper-city-farms/) grow the food at 0.6 g.
6. [The axis and the spine](/places/axis-and-spine/) is where you change from the turning city to the still tube that runs the length of the station.
7. [Ehricke, the industrial rotor](/places/industrial-rotor/) turns against the city and stores lunar and asteroid material.
8. [The zero-g shipyard](/places/shipyard/) builds 700 m ships in a vacuum yard.
9. [The spaceport](/places/spaceport/) is the passenger terminal, the piers and the propellant farm.
10. [Aft works](/places/aft-works/) lands the station's power and holds the reactors and thrusters.
11. [The exterior](/places/exterior/) is the hull, the radiators, the defences and the satellites.

{% shot "pl-exterior-hero" "The whole station in space, a grey cylinder with a caged ring section, radiator banks and attached utilities." %}
