## How these images are made

Art direction by David Veksler. Each painting starts from the engineering and ends as a graded image:

1. An engineering specification, written from NASA, SpaceX and other published sources.
2. A Blender 3D model of the habitat.
3. A three.js environment built from that model.
4. An in-game screenshot of the scene, the "model plate".
5. A painting pass: OpenAI img2img repaints the plate in the house style and keeps the architecture as built.
6. A people pass: figures are added at a set position in the painted plate, and nothing else changes.
7. Color grading.

Open any painting to look closer, or follow its place link to explore the setting.
