Pick a card and press **Open this view**. The simulation loads at that camera position in Explore mode. From there you can walk, fly or press `M` to choose another view.

Every card links to `/explore/?viewpoint=<id>`. You can paste the same link anywhere, or add `&overlay=gravity` to open the view with an engineering overlay on top. The [guide](/guide/) lists all the link options. To follow the story rather than browse, start with {% explore chapter="home" label="the family's breakfast" %} or read [the journey](/journey/).
