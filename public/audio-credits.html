Sound

Footsteps use recorded sounds from Footsteps on different surfaces, edited by congusbongus for C-Dogs SDL. Original recordings: concrete, grass and wood by swuing; aluminium ladder by Eelke; water footsteps by EminYILDIRIM. These recordings use CC BY 3.0. Road footsteps use Gravel Footsteps by Ali_6868 (CC0). Concordia trims, filters and loudness matches these samples. Download source, archive checksum and per-clip filenames are recorded in assets/audio/sfx-manifest.json.

Sound effects generated locally with Woosh by Sony AI (CC BY-NC 4.0); selected, edited and mixed for Concordia.

Tonal undertones generated locally with ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT); selected, edited and mixed for Concordia. ACE-Step on GitHub.

The racer pump whine was generated locally with Stable Audio Open 1.0 under its Stability AI Community License; selected, edited and mixed for Concordia.

Use of the Woosh clips is non-commercial. Concordia is a personal, non-monetized project, and the Woosh model weights are licensed for non-commercial use only. The Woosh sound files in this experience must not be reused in a commercial product. The ACE-Step undertones are MIT licensed.

Ambience, doors, vehicles and interface effects use text-prompted Woosh clips, with Stable Audio Open supplying the racer pump whine. The prompt and seed behind each clip are recorded in the project's sound manifest, so any clip can be regenerated. Editing was limited to trimming, fades, filtering, loudness matching and encoding to Opus and AAC. Room reverb tails are generated impulse responses applied in the browser. The tonal undertones are long beatless ACE-Step takes, each cut to a 60 s window with fades, filtered and loudness matched; their captions and seeds are in assets/audio/undertone-manifest.json.

The shared mix places the sounds by location: apartment, street, park, plaza, metro, ascender, axis, bearing gallery and vehicle cabins. Exterior views carry no diegetic sound by design; with the tonal layer switched on, a quiet undertone plays there and under the city.
