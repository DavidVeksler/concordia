Sound

Sound effects generated locally with Woosh by Sony AI (CC BY-NC 4.0); selected, edited and mixed for Concordia.

Tonal undertones generated locally with ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT); selected, edited and mixed for Concordia. ACE-Step on GitHub.

Use of the Woosh clips is non-commercial. Concordia is a personal, non-monetized project, and the Woosh model weights are licensed for non-commercial use only. The Woosh sound files in this experience must not be reused in a commercial product. The ACE-Step undertones are MIT licensed.

Every ambience bed, footstep, door, vehicle and interface sound is a text-prompted Woosh clip. The prompt and seed behind each clip are recorded in the project's sound manifest, so any clip can be regenerated. Editing was limited to trimming, slicing individual footsteps out of longer takes, fades, filtering, loudness matching and encoding to Opus and AAC. Room reverb tails are generated impulse responses applied in the browser. The tonal undertones are long beatless ACE-Step takes, each cut to a 60 s window with fades, filtered and loudness matched; their captions and seeds are in assets/audio/undertone-manifest.json.

The shared mix places the sounds by location: apartment, street, park, plaza, metro, ascender, axis, bearing gallery and vehicle cabins. Exterior views carry no diegetic sound by design; with the tonal layer switched on, a quiet undertone plays there and under the city.
