Earth, Moon & Sun imagery

Detailed Earth surface, Earth clouds and Sun surface maps: Solar System Scope / INOVE, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Adaptations: resized and recompressed to 2K/4K (Earth surface also 8K); cloud maps converted to grayscale opacity; Sun artwork rendered as luminance detail on a near-white emissive surface.

The Sun map is illustrative artwork, not a live solar observation. Earth cloud coverage is a static composite, not current weather.

Moon color and elevation: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit, Ernie Wright, NASA/LRO/LROC/LOLA. The 2019 LROC color mosaic is resized and recompressed; the LDEM16 elevation map is normalized to 8-bit height for surface relief. Used under NASA media usage guidelines. NASA does not endorse this experience.

Distant Earth: NASA Blue Marble. Distant Moon: NASA CGI Moon Kit. Distant Earth clouds are generated artwork.
