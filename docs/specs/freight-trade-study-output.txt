5. Angular momentum and the rotor ledger
====================================================================================================
Rotor angular momentum L = 1.174e+17 N m s (3e11 kg at 2500 m).
  100 kg tool     : lowering 500 -> 2500 m takes dL = 3.756e+07 N m s from the rotor (3.20e-10 of L); energy ledger: rotor gives m W^2 (r2^2-r1^2) = 0.65 kWh, of which the winch can recover half (0.33) and the cargo's inertial KE is the other half.
  1 t pallet      : lowering 500 -> 2500 m takes dL = 3.756e+08 N m s from the rotor (3.20e-09 of L); energy ledger: rotor gives m W^2 (r2^2-r1^2) = 6.53 kWh, of which the winch can recover half (3.27) and the cargo's inertial KE is the other half.
  10 t container  : lowering 500 -> 2500 m takes dL = 3.756e+09 N m s from the rotor (3.20e-08 of L); energy ledger: rotor gives m W^2 (r2^2-r1^2) = 65.31 kWh, of which the winch can recover half (32.66) and the cargo's inertial KE is the other half.
  50 t module     : lowering 500 -> 2500 m takes dL = 1.878e+10 N m s from the rotor (1.60e-07 of L); energy ledger: rotor gives m W^2 (r2^2-r1^2) = 326.56 kWh, of which the winch can recover half (163.28) and the cargo's inertial KE is the other half.
Net transfer: 1 Mt/yr moved from r=500 to the floor shifts the rotor by
  dL = 3.76e+14 N m s per year = 0.320 % of L per year; opposite-spin pairs and the momentum rings trim this (docs/15).
